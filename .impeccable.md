## Design Context

### Users

Business owners and staff use the EHRCMS business portal to register premises, manage food handlers, complete certificate journeys, and respond to council inspection notices and findings.

### Brand Personality

Calm, official, and focused. The interface should make regulatory steps feel clear and dependable.

### Aesthetic Direction

Keep the existing light, restrained portal language, readable typography, and measured use of the primary color. Business imagery should add identity and context without making the workflow feel promotional.

### Design Principles

- Show the current requirement, status, and next action plainly.
- Keep business actions distinct from clearly disclosed simulated external actions.
- Give forms room to breathe on desktop and a usable full-screen treatment on phones.
- Preserve direct links, browser navigation, and accessible controls across workflows.
- Use imagery to identify the business and premises; keep certificate and inspection content authoritative and easy to scan.
